## /practice-areas/criminal-defense

Retainer stated: **₩4,000,000 – ₩12,000,000** (matches Korean /fees "형사 변호" and /practice/criminal).

Procedural assertions:
- [ ] A Korean criminal case moves through: pre-interview → police interview → police decision whether to refer to the prosecutor → prosecutor's disposition → trial.
- [ ] Statements given at the police interview are recorded and carry through the rest of the case.
- [ ] A suspect may have counsel present at the police interview.
- [ ] A suspect may read the written record of their interview before signing and request corrections.
- [ ] Evidence and written statements can be submitted before the police refer/close decision.
- [ ] Sentencing material, restitution/settlement with a complainant, and written arguments can be submitted before the prosecutor's disposition.
- [ ] Prosecutors sometimes question suspects directly after referral.
- [ ] A departure restriction can be imposed during an investigation in some cases. ⚠️ (FAQ: "Can I leave Korea while the investigation is ongoing?")
- [ ] Police interviews are conducted in Korean, with interpretation arranged where needed. ⚠️
- [ ] A person is not obliged to answer questions in a way that incriminates them. ⚠️
- [ ] Summary orders exist and can be met with a request for a full trial (referenced in the "Is this you?" list).

Deliberately NOT asserted (do not add):
- Who is or is not notified of an investigation — the FAQ routes this to consultation and notes rules differ by status, including SOFA personnel.
- Any specific visa code, deportation threshold, or definite immigration consequence.

## /practice-areas/civil-litigation

Retainer stated: **from ₩3,000,000** (matches Korean /fees "민사 소송" and /practice/civil).

Procedural assertions:
- [ ] Sequence: pre-filing review → demand or payment order → filing and argument → judgment → enforcement.
- [ ] Limitation periods can bar a claim.
- [ ] Provisional attachment can be sought where there is a risk of assets being moved.
- [ ] A payment order becomes final if the other side does not object.
- [ ] Fact-finding enquiries and expert valuation can be applied for during litigation.
- [ ] There is **no legal route to inspect a debtor's assets before judgment**; asset disclosure and asset searches are only available after. ⚠️ (stated in FAQ and in "What We Will Tell You Honestly")
- [ ] Court costs generally follow the result, and attorney fees are recoverable only within limits set by court rules. ⚠️
- [ ] **30 days** to file an answer to a complaint, and a court may otherwise decide on the claimant's account alone. Same figures as the Korean /practice/civil page.
- [ ] **Two weeks** from receipt to oppose a payment order, after which it stands as final. Same figure as the Korean /practice/civil page.
- [ ] A judgment can be enforced later if the debtor acquires assets.

## /practice-areas/real-estate-lease-disputes

Retainer stated: **from ₩3,000,000** (matches Korean /fees "부동산" and /practice/real-estate).

Procedural assertions:
- [ ] Sequence: contract/facts review → protective measures → litigation → judgment and enforcement.
- [ ] Notice periods around renewal and termination **vary by contract type and circumstances**, so the page deliberately states no single figure. ⚠️
- [ ] Tenant protections attach to holding the property and to the registered address, and can be weakened by moving out and re-registering before securing the position. ⚠️
- [ ] A registration procedure exists for tenants who must move out before the deposit is returned, and it should be completed before moving. ⚠️ (the page does not name it — confirm whether it should)
- [ ] Housing lease protections are not restricted to Korean nationals. ⚠️
- [ ] A landlord may not recover possession by self-help (changing locks, removing belongings); possession goes through court process and enforcement. ⚠️
- [ ] Protective steps such as an injunction against transferring possession, provisional attachment, or a lease registration order are available depending on the dispute.
- [ ] A co-owner may seek division, and division will occur — the dispute is over method. ⚠️
- [ ] Deposit recovery involves ranking against other creditors (stated in "What We Will Tell You Honestly").

## /practice-areas/divorce-family-law

Retainer stated: **₩4,000,000 – ₩7,000,000** (matches Korean /fees "이혼" and /practice/divorce).

Procedural assertions:
- [ ] Sequence: preparation → divorce by agreement → filing and argument → mediation → judgment and compliance.
- [ ] Divorce by agreement proceeds with court confirmation.
- [ ] Courts commonly attempt mediation late in a contested case; a successful mediation ends the case with the same force as a final decision. ⚠️
- [ ] Applications to trace the other spouse's assets are available. ⚠️
- [ ] Protective/provisional orders can be sought during proceedings. ⚠️
- [ ] Property division is decided on contribution during the marriage rather than title, and contribution through housework and childcare is recognised. ⚠️
- [ ] Custody is decided on the child's circumstances; nationality is not the test. ⚠️
- [ ] Where support goes unpaid, a compliance order or enforcement against assets is available. ⚠️
- [ ] Jurisdiction can be genuinely in issue where spouses hold different nationalities or have lived in more than one country. ⚠️
- [ ] Consultation content is protected by an attorney's duty of confidentiality under Korean law. ⚠️
- [ ] Residency tied to a marriage is affected by divorce — stated as a real question whose answer depends on circumstances, **not** as a definite outcome. Confirm the wording is at an acceptable level of caution.
